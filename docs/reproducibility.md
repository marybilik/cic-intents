This document lists everything a reviewer needs to reproduce the
results in the paper. It follows the format recommended by the
[NeurIPS 2024 reproducibility checklist](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist)
and the Elsevier "Artifacts" requirements.

  - [`../notebooks/01-corpus-construction.ipynb`](../notebooks/01-corpus-construction.ipynb)

## 5. Evaluation protocols

### Main split

Single hold-out of 143 documents, stratified as above. Metrics computed
with threshold 0.5 for F1, and via full ranking for mAP / Top-k.

### Forum small-n

Three independent estimates, all reported:

- **Hold-out (`n = 17`):** the naive 70/15/15 contribution.
  Macro-F1 = **0.136**.
- **5-fold stratified CV (`n = 113`):** mean ± std across folds.
  Macro-F1 = **0.270 ± 0.064**, mAP = **0.504 ± 0.082**.
- **Leave-forum-out (`n = 113`):** model trained on email + SMS only,
  tested on all 113 forum documents.
  Macro-F1 = **0.228**, mAP = **0.338**.

The 4.2 pp gap between LOO (0.228) and CV (0.270) quantifies how much
forum-specific training helps.

### Bootstrap CIs

1000 resamples; 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. Important: for mAP, the
intent set is fixed across resamples (the same **nine** intents with
support ≥ 2 in the original test set). Otherwise, the two intents with
support = 1 would be excluded on many resamples, inflating the mean.

Reported CIs:

| Metric | Point estimate | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| mAP (macro) | 0.721 | [0.645, 0.806] |
| Macro-F1 | 0.465 | [0.419, 0.504] |
| Top-5 recall | 0.931 | [0.901, 0.957] |

### Paired tests

Paired bootstrap test with 1000 resamples, paired by example index.

## 6. Metric definitions

- **mAP (macro):** mean of Average Precision over the nine intents with
  test-set support ≥ 2.
- **Macro-F1:** unweighted mean of per-intent F1 at threshold 0.5.
- **Top-k recall:** fraction of ground-truth intents appearing in the
  top-k predicted intents, averaged over documents with ≥ 1 true intent.
- **Domain MI:** **normalized** mutual information between channel
  labels and predictions of a 5-fold cross-validated logistic-regression
  probe on the CLS embeddings.
- **PN (Probability of Necessity):** `PN(t) = f_t(x) - f_t(x \ T_k(x))`.
- **PS (Probability of Sufficiency):** `PS(t) = f_t(T_k(x)) - f_t(x \ T_k(x))`.

Where `x` is the input text, `T_k(x)` is the set of top-k tokens ranked
by gradient-based importance, and `f_t(·)` is the sigmoid probability of
intent `t`.

## 12. Known issues

- **`hist_main` not retained:** the main model's training history was
  not saved during the original run. Figure 1 shows the LOO-forum
  variant instead. We document this in the figure caption.
- **Forum small-n:** the naive 70/15/15 split gives only 17 test
  documents for the forum channel, which produces unstable estimates.
  We mitigate this with 5-fold CV and leave-forum-out. All three
  numbers are reported.
- **Telegram proxy:** no legitimate Italian fraud corpus for Telegram
  exists; we use a non-hateful subset of MuLTa-Telegram as a proxy and
  document this as a limitation.
- **Two PCC protocols:** the headline number uses train-means → test
  (0.676 → 0.116, −82.9%). The ablation uses test-means
  (0.676 → 0.442 for the full model; 0.788 → 0.380 for BCE-only).
  Both are reported in the paper.
